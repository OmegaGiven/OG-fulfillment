Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

OG Fulfillment ("we", "our", or "us") operates the OG Fulfillment mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password, or your Apple ID token if you sign in with Apple. We do not receive or store your Apple password.

Integration Credentials

To connect e-commerce platforms (e.g. Shopify, eBay, Etsy, Amazon), you provide API keys or access tokens. These credentials are stored only on your device, in secure platform storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us or any third party.

Order and Fulfillment Data

The App fetches order data from connected platforms on your behalf. This data (order numbers, recipient names, shipping addresses, product details) is displayed to you and stored locally on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, it is stored in your private Firestore collection, accessible only to your account.

Photos and Camera

The App requests camera access to photograph shipping labels for OCR scanning. It may also access your photo library to attach product images to outgoing customer emails. Photos used for scanning are processed on-device and are not uploaded to our servers. If you enable Photo Backup (a paid feature), photos are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage under your account and are only accessible to you.

Subscription and Purchase Information

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated user ID and your purchase receipt from the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and Crash Data

We do not currently use third-party analytics or crash reporting services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account
  • To sync your data across devices (if cloud sync is enabled)
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To back up photos to your private cloud storage (if Photo Backup is enabled)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Local data is stored on your device using secure platform storage. Cloud data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) and is protected by Firebase security rules that restrict access to your authenticated account only. Data in transit is encrypted via TLS.

4. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete the App. Cloud data associated with your account is retained until you delete your account. You can request deletion by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
